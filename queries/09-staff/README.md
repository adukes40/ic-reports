# Staff

Staff credentials, demographics, assignments and rosters.

Open a query, click **Copy content** (top right of the file), and paste it into IC's data extract tool.

| # | Query | Status | What it finds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 108 | [Staff demographics blank](04-staff-demographics-blank.sql) | Not run yet | Active staff whose current identity has no race, Hispanic/Latino answer, gender or birthdate. |
| 109 | [Assignment / employment date conflicts](05-assignment-employment-date-conflicts.sql) | Not run yet | District assignments still open after the person's employment ended, and district assignments or employment records with no start date. |
| 110 | [Staff scheduled with no active assignment](06-staff-scheduled-no-active-assignment.sql) | Not run yet | Staff on this year's sections who have no district assignment open on @asOf at the school where they are scheduled. |
