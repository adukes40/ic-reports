# Operational lists

The lists offices, counselors and principals ask for every week.

Open a query, click **Copy content** (top right of the file), and paste it into IC's data extract tool.

| # | Query | Status | What it finds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 118 | [New enrollees and withdrawals](01-new-enrollees-and-withdrawals.sql) | Not run yet | Everyone who entered or left a school in a date range, with school, grade and the start or end status. Used by front offices, counselors and nurses. |
| 119 | [Early-warning ABC roster](02-early-warning-abc-roster.sql) | Not run yet | Active students flagged on attendance (10%+ of days absent), behavior (2+ referrals) or course performance (1+ F; English or math F in grades 6-8) this year. Used by principals and MTSS teams. |
| 120 | [Counselor caseload](03-counselor-caseload.sql) | Not run yet | Each counselor's assigned active students with grade, attendance rate, D/F count and referral count this year. Used by counselors. |
| 121 | [Custody and no-contact flags](04-custody-and-no-contact-flags.sql) | Not run yet | Active students with a current court order, custody, restricted pickup or no-contact flag, by school, showing the flag type only. Used by front offices. |
| 122 | [Transportation roster](05-transportation-roster.sql) | Not run yet | Active students with their bus number, stops and times and home address. Students with no transportation record are listed as a separate group. Used by the transportation office. |
| 123 | [Sibling / household list](06-sibling-household-list.sql) | Not run yet | Households with active students in more than one school, with each student's school and grade. Used by offices planning mailings, events and conferences. |
| 124 | [Birthday list](07-birthday-list.sql) | Not run yet | Active students with a birthday in the chosen month, by school, with name, grade and day of the month. Used by offices and principals for announcements. |
| 125 | [Portal adoption](08-portal-adoption.sql) | Not run yet | Per school, how many guardians of active students have a parent portal account and how many logged in during the last @days days. Used by principals and the tech office. |
| 126 | [Fees owed](09-fees-owed.sql) | Not run yet | Active students with an open fee balance, with the fee name, amount owed and the date the fee was assigned. Fees from earlier years that are still unpaid are included. Used by school offices and bookkeepers. |
| 127 | [Mailing labels / mail merge](10-mailing-labels-mail-merge.sql) | Not run yet | One mailing label per household for active students in the chosen school and grades: guardians, students and mailing address. Used by school offices for letters and mail merges. |
| 128 | [Summer school roster](11-summer-school-roster.sql) | Not run yet | Students enrolled in a summer school calendar with grade, home school, guardian phone numbers and whether they have an active IEP, 504 plan or EL status. Used by summer school staff and nurses. |
