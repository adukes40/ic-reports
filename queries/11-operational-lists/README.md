# Operational lists

The lists offices, counselors and principals ask for every week.

Open a query, click **Copy content** (top right of the file), and paste it into IC's data extract tool.

| # | Query | Status | What it finds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 118 | [New enrollees and withdrawals](01-new-enrollees-and-withdrawals.sql) | Not run yet | Everyone who entered or left a school in a date range, with school, grade and the start or end status. Used by front offices, counselors and nurses. |
| 119 | [Early-warning ABC roster](02-early-warning-abc-roster.sql) | Not run yet | Active students flagged on attendance (10%+ of days absent), behavior (2+ referrals) or course performance (1+ F; English or math F in grades 6-8) this year. Used by principals and MTSS teams. |
| 120 | [Counselor caseload](03-counselor-caseload.sql) | Not run yet | Each counselor's assigned active students with grade, attendance rate, D/F count and referral count this year. Used by counselors. |
| 121 | [Custody and no-contact flags](04-custody-and-no-contact-flags.sql) | Not run yet | Active students with a current court order, custody, restricted pickup or no-contact flag, by school, showing the flag type only. Used by front offices. |
| 122 | [Transportation roster](05-transportation-roster.sql) | Not run yet | Active students with their bus number, stops and times and home address. Students with no transportation record are listed as a separate group. Used by the transportation office. |
