# Delaware sources

The Delaware rules the queries in this library follow, with the query that applies each one. Read the rule itself before relying on a result for state reporting: regulations change, and a query only reflects the rule as it stood when the query was written.

Last checked: November 2026.

| Topic | What the rule says | Source | Queries |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Unit count** | The count window is the last 10 school days of September. A student who was absent all 10 days can still be counted with a documented reason, the date of last contact, and a reason to expect a return before November 1. Students with disabilities need an IEP in effect during the last week of school in September. A district enrolling an in-state transfer during the window must notify the previous district by the last student day of September. | [14 DE Admin. Code 701](https://regulations.delaware.gov/AdminCode/title14/700/701.shtml), sections 1.3, 3.1, 4.1.10, 5.1.1 | [#1 Unit count readiness](queries/01-delaware-ddoe/01-unit-count-readiness.sql), [#2 Unit count transfers](queries/01-delaware-ddoe/02-unit-count-transfers.sql) |
| **Truancy** | A student is truant after more than 3 unexcused days in a school year, which triggers a truancy conference. The principal must refer the case for prosecution after the 20th unexcused day. | [14 Del. C. ch. 27, subch. II](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title14/c027/sc02/index.html), §2721 and §2725 | [#8 Truancy ladder](queries/01-delaware-ddoe/08-truancy-ladder.sql) |
| **Attendance policy** | Each district sets its own attendance policy, including what counts as excused. A hard copy of the policy goes home once a student passes 10 unexcused absences. Compulsory attendance covers ages 5 to 16. | [14 DE Admin. Code 615](https://regulations.delaware.gov/AdminCode/title14/600/615.shtml), section 3.3; [14 Del. C. §2702](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title14/c027/sc01/index.html) | [#8 Truancy ladder](queries/01-delaware-ddoe/08-truancy-ladder.sql) |
| **IEP transition** | Transition planning starts with the first IEP in effect when the student turns 14 or enters grade 8, whichever comes first. | [14 DE Admin. Code 925](https://regulations.delaware.gov/AdminCode/title14/900/925.shtml), section 7.2 | [#12 SPED Indicator 13 / IEP compliance](queries/01-delaware-ddoe/12-sped-indicator-13-iep-compliance.sql) |
| **IEP review and re-evaluation** | An IEP is reviewed at least once a year. A re-evaluation happens at least every 3 years unless the parent and the district agree it is unnecessary. An initial evaluation is finished within 45 school days or 90 calendar days of consent, whichever is less. | [14 DE Admin. Code 925](https://regulations.delaware.gov/AdminCode/title14/900/925.shtml), sections 2.3, 3.2, 11.7 | [#81 IEP / evaluation due dates](queries/07-special-programs/03-iep-evaluation-due-dates.sql) |
| **Removals of students with IEPs** | After a student with a disability has been removed for 10 school days in a school year, services must be provided during any further removal. A removal of more than 10 consecutive school days is a change of placement. | [14 DE Admin. Code 926](https://regulations.delaware.gov/AdminCode/title14/900/926.shtml), section 30.0 | [#82 Discipline days for students with IEPs](queries/07-special-programs/04-discipline-days-students-with-ieps.sql) |
| **Suspension and expulsion** | A short-term suspension is at least half a school day and no more than 10 consecutive school days. A long-term suspension is 11 consecutive school days or more. | [14 DE Admin. Code 616](https://regulations.delaware.gov/AdminCode/title14/600/616.shtml), definitions | [#102 Exclusionary discipline days](queries/08-behavior-discipline/10-exclusionary-discipline-days.sql) |
| **Restraint and seclusion** | The school must send a written report of a physical restraint to the Department of Education within 72 hours. | [14 DE Admin. Code 610](https://regulations.delaware.gov/AdminCode/title14/600/610.shtml), section 6.1.2 | [#104 Restraint / seclusion incidents](queries/08-behavior-discipline/12-restraint-seclusion-incidents.sql) |
| **English learners** | English learners take the ACCESS assessment every year. After a student is reclassified, the district monitors academic performance for at least two school years. | [14 DE Admin. Code 920](https://regulations.delaware.gov/AdminCode/title14/900/920.shtml), section 4.0 | [#86 EL monitoring years](queries/07-special-programs/08-el-monitoring-years.sql) |
| **Graduation requirements** | 24 credits: 4 English language arts, 4 mathematics, 3 science, 3 social studies, 2 world language, 1 physical education, ½ health, 3 career pathway and 3½ electives. One mathematics credit is earned in the senior year. | [14 DE Admin. Code 505](https://regulations.delaware.gov/AdminCode/title14/500/505.shtml), section 5.1 | [#70 Seniors not on track to graduate](queries/06-grades-transcripts/06-seniors-not-on-track-to-graduate.sql) |
| **Immunizations** | Before grade 9: a Tdap booster and one dose of meningococcal vaccine, with a meningococcal booster at age 16 to 18. Families have 14 calendar days after notice to show the series has started. Religious exemptions are renewed every year. | [14 DE Admin. Code 804](https://regulations.delaware.gov/AdminCode/title14/800/804.shtml), sections 4.1, 5.2, 7.1.3 | [#14 Immunization compliance](queries/01-delaware-ddoe/14-immunization-compliance.sql), [#113 Grade-entry boosters due](queries/10-health/02-grade-entry-boosters-due.sql) |
| **Health examinations** | One exam within the 2 years before a student first enrolls in a Delaware public school, and a second within the 2 years before grade 9. Students have 30 calendar days to comply. | [14 DE Admin. Code 815](https://regulations.delaware.gov/AdminCode/title14/800/815.shtml), section 3.1 | [#114 School health exam forms missing](queries/10-health/03-school-health-exam-forms-missing.sql) |
| **Screenings** | Vision and hearing in kindergarten and grades 2, 4, 7 and 9 or 10, by January 15. Postural and gait in grades 5 to 9, by December 15. Lead screening documentation for kindergarten enterers, and an oral health screening in kindergarten. | [14 DE Admin. Code 815](https://regulations.delaware.gov/AdminCode/title14/800/815.shtml), section 4.0 | [#116 Screenings due](queries/10-health/05-screenings-due.sql) |

## Accountability measures

These measures are defined in Department of Education guidance rather than in regulation. The queries below follow the published summaries; check them against the current guidance before using the numbers.

| Measure | What the queries assume | Guidance | Queries |
|---|---|---|---|
| **On Track Attendance (chronic absence)** | A student is chronically absent after missing 10% or more of enrolled days. Full-day absences count, excused or not, and what makes a full day is set by local board policy. | [On Track Attendance guidance](https://education.delaware.gov/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/On-Track-Attendance-Guidance-Document-2021-2022.pdf), [Chronic absenteeism resource guide](https://education.delaware.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/190829_chronic_absenteeism_resource_guide_final.pdf) | [#7 On-Track Attendance (DSSF chronic absence)](queries/01-delaware-ddoe/07-on-track-attendance-dssf.sql), [#54 Chronic absence list + rate](queries/05-attendance/02-chronic-absence-list-rate.sql) |
| **On Track in 9th Grade** | A first-time 9th grader is on track with 4 or more credits spread across at least four of English language arts, mathematics, science, social studies and world languages. | [DSSF technical manual](https://education.delaware.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/DSSF_Tech-Manual_10_12_22.pdf), [Student On-Track in 9th Grade dataset](https://data.delaware.gov/Education/Student-On-Track-in-9th-Grade/ip48-pwtb) | [#6 On Track in 9th Grade](queries/01-delaware-ddoe/06-on-track-9th-grade.sql) |

More on the framework: [Measuring school performance](https://education.delaware.gov/educators/school-operations/measuring_school_performance).
